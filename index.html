The Challenge

Standing out in a saturated market.

To expand the market, Revolve was launching a new electric model, positioning as a premium, domestic portable oven featuring an innovative smart heating element.

Because the B2C website serves as Revolve's primary sales channel, we needed to deliberately redesign the digital experience to clearly differentiate our technology.

Execution

Research & Workshops

Data & User Research

Quantitative Research: Analysed Shopify and Google Analytics data (traffic sources, cart abandonment rate etc.) to pinpoint user drop-offs.

Qualitative Research: Launched an incentivized survey on Instagram & Monday.com, offering a €10 voucher to uncover exactly what features pizza lovers prioritize when purchasing their ovens.

Miro Wireframing Workshops

Facilitated two crucial workshops with stakeholders:

A Discovery Workshop to align on core feature priorities based on assumptions and data.

A Prototype Workshop to visualize the new information architecture and user journey.

Project Management

Agile Sprint Planning

The development phase was a strict 1-month timeline. The team structured the workflow into highly focused 2-week sprints to maintain momentum and ensure quality deliverables.

1. Setting Tasks

Assigning task for team on Trello, setting priorities, lables, dealdlines, and defining sprint goals.

2. One-Week Sprint

Intensive one-week development cycles focusing on core Shopify implementations and continuous alignment.

3. Review & Unblock

Conducted sprint review sessions, dedicating the following week entirely to clearing blockers and refining QA items.

Lifestyle & Community Driven

78% of our target audience prioritizes a "family-oriented" and "community-based" cooking lifestyle. The hierarchy reflects this by emphasizing emotional hero imagery and UGC community blocks.

Credibility for High-Ticket Items

Premium pizza ovens represent a significant financial investment - Positioning authoritative "Social Proof" below the products establishes immediate credibility before users scroll away.

Ecosystem Cross-Selling

Integrated Revolve Accessories and Revolve Academy towards the footer, the site seamlessly transitions from a one-time product pitch into an ongoing ecosystem.

Risk Management

Critical Path Blockers & Solutions

Alignment Drift

Many project details were communicated on Slack without definitive conclusions, which led to misaligned expectations and caused us to waste critical development hours in repetitive alignment meetings.

PM Solution: The team established a "Agenda-and-Outcome" meeting guideline and migrated all action items from Slack into a centralized Decision Log on Trello to restore clarity and velocity.

Blocker 02: Asset Pipeline

The Situation: Delayed 3D renders stalled front-end development, risking a milestone delay.

UX Solution: Designed structural placeholders, allowing developers to code independently of the delayed media assets.

Retrospective Takeaway

I've found that proactive dependency mapping is just as critical as UX wireframing. By establishing centralized communication protocols and building decoupled architectural structures, I can ensure my projects maintain momentum even when external blockers occur.
